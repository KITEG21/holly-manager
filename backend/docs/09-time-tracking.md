# Time tracking

Time can be logged in three ways: from the task panel, from the timesheet ("Add time"), or with the timer.

## Timesheet

**Timesheet** shows your hours for one week (Monday to Sunday) as a grid of tasks by day, with a total per task, per day and for the week. Use the arrows to move between weeks; you cannot go past the current week. Today's column is highlighted, and days above 8 hours are shown in amber. Click a task to open its panel.

"Add time" opens a form with the task, the day, the hours and an optional note. Only open tasks without pending dependencies are listed.

## Timer

Start the timer from a task panel. It appears in the top bar with the task and the elapsed time, and keeps running if you navigate or reload the page. Stopping it logs the time rounded to the nearest quarter of an hour, with a minimum of 15 minutes, and the note "Timer".

## Rules for time entries

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Hours per entry | More than 0 and at most 24 |
| Hours per day | At most 24 hours per person per day across all entries |
| Dates | Not in the future, and at most 60 days back |
| Deleting | You can delete your own entries; admins can delete anyone's. Deleting an entry lowers the task's actual hours |

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** summarises your work: hours today, hours this week against a weekly target, your open tasks, the tasks closed by the team this week, your next tasks (in-progress first), the health of every project and recent team activity.

The weekly target is capacity per sprint ÷ team members ÷ sprint length in weeks. With the defaults (180 h, 3 people, 2 weeks) it is 30 hours.
