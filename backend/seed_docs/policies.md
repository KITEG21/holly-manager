# Commercial policies

## Methodology

We work in two-week sprints. At the end of each sprint we hold a demo with the client and reprioritise the backlog within the agreed scope.

## Communication

Every project has an account manager. We reply to messages within one business day at most. Follow-up meetings are weekly and last no more than 30 minutes.

## Technology and hosting

By default we deploy to the client's cloud. If the client has no infrastructure of their own, we can manage it for them; the cost of cloud services is invoiced separately at cost price.

## Maintenance after support

When the included support period ends, we offer an optional monthly maintenance plan, contracted separately.

## Data protection

We sign a data processing agreement when the project handles personal data. Production data is only used in production environments.
