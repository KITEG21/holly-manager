# Software development services agreement

## 1. Purpose

The company providing the service (hereinafter, "the Provider") will develop for the Client the software described in the confirmed Requirements Document, in the version identified in the proposal. Any functionality not included in that document falls outside the purpose of this agreement.

## 2. Price and payment terms

The total price is the one stated in the attached financial proposal. Payment will be made through a deposit on signature and milestone payments according to the schedule in the proposal. Invoices will be paid by bank transfer within a maximum of 30 days from their issue date. Payment delays of more than 15 days allow the Provider to suspend work until they are settled.

## 3. Timeline

The estimated delivery time is stated in the proposal and is organised in fixed-length sprints. Timelines start counting from receipt of the deposit and from the Client providing the necessary access and materials. Delays attributable to the Client extend the timeline by the same amount.

## 4. Change management

The price includes the number of change rounds stated in the proposal. A change round is a set of adjustments to functionality already included in the scope, communicated all at once. New functionality or out-of-scope changes will be handled through a change request, which will include its impact on price and timeline and will require the Client's written approval.

## 5. Acceptance

At the end of each milestone, the Client will have 10 business days to validate the delivery against the acceptance criteria in the Requirements Document. If no issues are reported within that period, the delivery will be considered accepted.

## 6. Intellectual property

Once the total price has been paid, the Client will own the exploitation rights to the code developed specifically for the project. The Provider retains ownership of its libraries, tools and pre-existing components, over which it grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual licence of use.

## 7. Warranty and support

The Provider will fix, at no cost, any defects that prevent the acceptance criteria from being met during the support period stated in the proposal, counted from the final delivery. Support does not include new functionality or changes caused by third-party modifications.

## 8. Confidentiality

Both parties undertake not to disclose the other party's confidential information during the term of the agreement and for the following three years.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement for serious breach by the other, after a written notice given 15 days in advance. In the event of termination, the Client will pay for the work carried out up to that date.
